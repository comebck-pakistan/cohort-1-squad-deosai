    06 — Cross-Cutting Findings

    Faster replies, fewer lost sales

    The clearest signal across both tracks: reply speed tracks directly against lost sales.

    Jewellery with Maheen ~1 hr reply 0 lost sales · 50+ parcels
    ZayZaiwar (Shamoon) 30 min – 1 hr reply No lost-sale incident
    StickerMania 1–2 days reply Lost 2 sales to a missed DM
    • Availability & price dominate even when a catalogue already exists — 8 of 12 name Product Questions first, echoed by StickerMania, Maheen & ZayZaiwar.
    • After-hours gaps cost real sales — StickerMania's 2 lost customers came from a single missed notification.
    • Segment by product type: catalogue-driven sellers fit the full assistant; custom/bespoke sellers (AP Crafts) fit a lighter closed-hours/policy responder.
